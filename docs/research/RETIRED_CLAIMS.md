`tests/test_docs_retired_claims.py` scans for across `docs/**/*.md` and `CLAUDE.md`.
**Before quoting a number in any doc, grep this file.**

Rules the test applies:

  plus the per-row "also allowed" files (the correcting source).
* A hit is allowed on a line that states the correction — it matches the row's
  "exempt if line has" regex, or the global exemption
  `retir|correct|supersed|\[X\]|was |wrong|false|instead of|not |≠|before|pre-fix|retract` (case-insensitive).
  This is the sweep's rule: a retired value may be said only as the "before" of a stated correction.
* Patterns are Python regexes, case-insensitive; `\|` in a cell means alternation.
